## About

Seventh-semester Biomedical Engineering student at **Universidad Antonio Nariño** (Popayán, Colombia), currently on a **fully funded academic exchange** at **Universidad Estatal de Sonora** (Hermosillo, Mexico). Expected graduation: **2027**.

I work at the intersection of **physiological signals, medical imaging and machine learning** — the part of biomedical engineering where a noisy measurement from a real body has to become a decision a clinician can trust. That means I care equally about the algorithm and about the hardware that produced the data, so I move between three layers:

- **Signals, sound &amp; imaging** — filtering, feature extraction and classification of biomedical data: electrocardiographic and phonocardiographic signals, and medical images.
- **Deep learning** — PyTorch and TensorFlow models for classification and segmentation, with an emphasis on validating them honestly rather than reporting a single lucky accuracy.
- **Instrumentation &amp; prototyping** — Arduino/ESP32 acquisition circuits and Fusion 360 design for enclosures, fixtures and printable device parts.

I write and speak **Spanish (native)** and read technical English fluently.

## Undergraduate Thesis · in progress (2026 – 2027)

I am building a **cardiac simulation platform for clinical training**. It brings together synthetic generation of cardiac signals, deep learning models for **electrocardiographic and phonocardiographic** pattern recognition, and a physical electromechanical device that reproduces the behaviour so that students and clinical staff can practise on it.

The work is ongoing and not yet defended, so implementation details and source code are not public. Happy to discuss the approach directly with researchers, potential collaborators or hiring teams.
